Privacy policy

Effective 18 October 2026

This policy explains how the Sur app for Android and iOS (“Sur”, “the app”, “we”, “us”) handles information. The short version: Sur does not collect, store or share any personal data.

How the microphone is used

Sur needs microphone access to measure the pitch of your instrument. While tuning is switched on:

  • Audio is processed in memory on your device, in short windows of about 85 milliseconds, to calculate a frequency and a deviation in cents.
  • Each window is discarded as soon as it has been analysed. Sur never writes audio to a file and never sends audio, or anything derived from it, off your device.
  • The microphone is active only after you tap Start tuning, and stops when you tap Stop or leave the app.

Information we collect

None. Sur has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email, contacts, location or any other personal information. It contains no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking software, and it does not use device identifiers to follow you across apps or websites.

Settings on your device

Choices you make in the app, such as tuner mode or a locked string, may be kept on your device so the app remembers them. They stay on your device and are removed when you uninstall Sur.

Optional purchases

Sur may offer optional in-app purchases, such as a tip or a Pro upgrade. These are processed entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store under their own terms and privacy policies. We never receive your card or bank details. The store tells the app only whether a purchase was made, so it can unlock what you paid for.

If a future version adds any service that handles purchase records or other data, we will update this policy before that version is released.

Permissions

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO on Android, Microphone on iOS): required for tuning, used only as described above.

You can turn microphone access off at any time in your phone’s settings. Tuning will not work without it, but nothing else changes.

Children

Sur is suitable for players of all ages. Because the app collects no personal data from anyone, it collects none from children.

Your rights

Data protection laws, including India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the GDPR, give you rights to access, correct and delete your personal data. Since Sur holds no personal data about you, there is nothing to access or delete. You are still welcome to contact us with any question or concern, and we will respond within 30 days.
